description: Get the idea, one real moment, and what would change, in a single message; then show the problem, user, need, intervention, and impact metric back once for the founder to fix.
trigger: Idea
heading: IDEA (stage 1 of 3)

Open with "Stage 1 of 3: Idea." In the same message, ask only the questions the founder's words so far have not answered, each as "Question n:" numbered from 1 in the order below, with no example or framing sentence under them:

- What's the idea, and who is it for?
- The last time you saw one of those people hit this problem: what happened, and what did it cost them?
- If this worked, what would be different for them six months from now?

Then this line, verbatim: "Answer in one reply. Rough is fine. Specifics beat length: one real example, any numbers, and what you already have."

If the answer to 2 is general rather than one real moment ("founders struggle to pitch"), ask once, as one "Question:" line and nothing else, for the last specific time it happened. That is the only follow-up. Anything still thin becomes a guess in the recap, where the founder fixes it in one reply. Never ask a leading question, never pitch inside one.

Do not ask where people would use it or what data the founder has. Setup covers both.

Then the recap, the first place you restate anything, with no lead-in sentence. One line per field, in the founder's vocabulary, with anything you inferred marked "(guess)":

- **Problem.** One or two concrete sentences.
- **User.** Who and where, one phrase ("middle school science teachers in coastal Texas"). No behaviors.
- **Unmet need.** What the user lacks.
- **If-then.** "If ..., then ...". The "then" half names the change in the user's life or work, not what the product does. "If teachers can localize a lesson in minutes, then localized lessons exist" fails. "... then they teach climate topics their students recognize" passes.
- **Impact metric.** Each metric the founder gave, with its rung in a few words: **reach** (who shows up), **behavior** (what users do differently), or **outcome** (what changes in their life or situation). Never treat their choice as wrong. If none is an outcome, offer two or three outcome metrics drawn from their product as lettered options.

End with "Reply ok, or fix any line" (and "plus a letter" if you offered metrics). When they reply, start AI jobs in the next message without commenting on their answer.
